Who I am

I'm Timileyin, a fullstack developer based in Ibadan, Nigeria. Before moving into fullstack development, I learned and worked with WordPress, building a strong understanding of content-driven websites, practical client needs, and how people use the web.

From there, I moved into custom development with React and Node.js. The projects on this page include collaborative boards, voting platforms, tournament experiences, e-commerce products, and real-time applications — built with modern JavaScript, Firebase, and fullstack tooling.

I'm currently learning TypeScript to make the systems I build more reliable, maintainable, and easier to scale. I also work with n8n when automation can make a product or workflow more useful.
